What to do immediately

👤

Keep the victim calm and reassure them.

🦿

Immobilize the affected limb using a splint.

⏱️

Remove tight items (rings, bracelets, anklets).

⛔

Do NOT cut, suck or apply traditional substances.

🚑

Transport to the nearest health facility quickly.
